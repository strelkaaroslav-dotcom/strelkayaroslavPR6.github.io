Agridera Fresh Market Carrot Hybrids

Agridera aims to be one of the world's leading companies in the breeding, production, and marketing of carrot seeds. We invest intensively in research and development to provide solutions to growers and consumers in numerous countries around the world.
In particular, we breed improved disease-resistant cultivars adapted to different markets globally.

Currently, emphasis is placed on combining resistance to Alternaria Leaf Blight and Cavity Spot with other soil-borne diseases into superior carrot breeding lines and hybrids. Comparative efforts are also undertaken to develop varieties resistant to root-knot nematodes.

In terms of horticultural traits, breeding focuses on root shape, uniform color, core size, sweetness, firmness, crack resistance, shelf life, and high/low temperature adaptation. Varieties range from Nantes, Chantenay, Danvers, Imperator, and Paris Market types.

Agridera Carrot breeding programs focus on three main segments: Fresh Market Carrots, Processing Carrots, and Specialty / Baby Carrots.

Fresh Market Carrot Program

Aimed at European and Middle Eastern markets. Focus on Nantes and Chantenay types with excellent color uniformity, high sugar content, and resistances to Alternaria and Cavity Spot.

Processing Carrot Program

For industrial processing in Northern and Eastern Europe. Imperator and Danvers types with high dry matter content, deep orange color, strong vigor, and excellent post-harvest shelf life.

Specialty & Baby Carrot Program

For premium and gourmet markets worldwide. Paris Market round types and mini Nantes for containers, raised beds, and specialty retail. High sweetness, tender flesh, and attractive appearance.

Agridera operates through international and local distributers around the world.
